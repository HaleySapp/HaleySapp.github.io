Privacy Policy

MirrorStat TCG • Last Updated September 25, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how MirrorStat TCG handles information when you use the app.

Information Collection

MirrorStat TCG does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the app.

MirrorStat TCG does not independently collect or store personal information on external MirrorStat servers.

Game Data and Saved Settings

Game information, layouts, preferences, and other settings created within MirrorStat TCG are intended for use within the app and may be stored locally on the user's device.

MirrorStat TCG does not operate an external account system for storing this information.

In-App Purchases

MirrorStat TCG may offer optional in-app purchases, including MirrorStat Pro.

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit services. MirrorStat TCG does not directly receive or store users' payment card information.

Advertising and Tracking

MirrorStat TCG does not use third-party advertising networks and does not use user information for cross-app advertising tracking.

Third-Party Services

MirrorStat TCG uses Apple platform services where necessary to provide app functionality, including App Store services for purchases.

Use of Apple services may be subject to Apple's own privacy practices and policies.

Children's Privacy

MirrorStat TCG does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through an account or registration system.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because MirrorStat TCG does not operate user accounts or external servers for storing users' personal information, MirrorStat TCG does not maintain an external personal-data profile for users to request deletion of.

Locally stored app information can generally be removed by deleting the relevant information within the app or by removing the app from the device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if MirrorStat TCG's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
